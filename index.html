00. THE GENESIS

The evolution of STEM Racing.

STEM Racing is a global movement where physics meets the speed of Formula 1. This platform is the Digital Twin for Team N!TRO—a hub for partners and judges to evaluate our technical depth as we prepare for 2026.

A Legacy of Speed

Originating from the F1 in Schools challenge, STEM Racing has evolved from a classroom project into a professional-grade engineering circuit. It mimics the real-world Formula 1 lifecycle: from initial sketches to CFD and final manufacturing.

The Technical Frontier

Over the last decade, the competition has shifted toward advanced aerospace-grade simulations. Our team utilizes cloud computing and AI-driven generative design to find millisecond advantages on the track.

01 · ABOUT

Built as one.

Team N!TRO represents The British School, New Delhi in STEM Racing. We develop a miniature CO₂-powered race car through research, design, analysis, manufacture, testing, branding and presentation.

After merging with Energise and Radeon, the new N!TRO brings together five specialists with one shared standard: every detail must have a reason.

02 · ENGINEERING

ORB!TUS DNA.

Our previous race car established the engineering base for what comes next.

BEARINGS

BOCA hybrids

BODY

Polyurethane foam

WHEELS

7-spoke SLS nylon

FINAL MASS

51 g

RACE MODEL

1.088 s

MANUFACTURING

CNC + MJF PA12

BOCA HYBRID BEARINGS

Low rolling resistance. High consistency.

Hybrid bearings combine ceramic balls with steel races, giving ORB!TUS a durable, low-friction wheel system designed for repeatable race runs.

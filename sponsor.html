Team N!TRO represents The British School, New Delhi in STEM Racing (formerly F1 in Schools). In our first season we finished 3rd of 57 at the Regional Finals, 10th of 93 at the National Finals and won the Team Identity Award. Now N!TRO, Energise and Radeon have merged into one team building G-ORB!T — and we're looking for partners to build it with us.
